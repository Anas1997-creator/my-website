Frequently asked questions

Is the walk-thru really free?

Yes. We visit your home, review what needs attention and explain your options. There's no obligation to join.

Do I have to be an L. Brooke Homes client?

HomeCare360 is designed for L. Brooke Homes homeowners first. If you're not sure, submit the form and we'll let you know.

What if I need a specialist, like HVAC or roofing?

We coordinate licensed specialists and manage the job, so you only deal with one team.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. If it isn't the right fit in the first 30 days, we refund your unused visits.

How quickly will someone contact me?

A coordinator will reach out within one business day of your request.

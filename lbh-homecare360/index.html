Frequently asked questions

What is HomeCare360?

A home care membership from L. Brooke Homes. We schedule seasonal upkeep, keep the record of what was done, and give you one place to see what is coming.

Do I have to be an L. Brooke Homes client?

HomeCare360 is designed for L. Brooke Homes homeowners first. If you're not sure, send the form and we'll let you know.

What if I need a specialist, like HVAC or roofing?

We coordinate trade partners we trust and manage the job, so you deal with one team.

How quickly will someone contact me?

A coordinator will reach out within one business day of your request.

What happens in an emergency?

Emergency support is available 24/7 all year on our fullest plan.
